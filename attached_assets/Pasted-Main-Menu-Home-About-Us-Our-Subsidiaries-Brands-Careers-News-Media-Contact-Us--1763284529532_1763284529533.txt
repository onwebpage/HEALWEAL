2. About Us
Company Overview

Healweal Corp is India’s fastest growing tech conglomerate and a parent company to 5 subsidiaries named Hapdax, Sachna, Kepwe, Prosmer and HAt.

Vision, Mission & Values
VISION

To empower people to live healthier and wealthier lives through innovation, trust, and intelligent technology.

MISSION

To redefine how the world experiences health and wealth — by creating intelligent platforms that bring wellbeing, opportunity, and prosperity within everyone’s reach.
